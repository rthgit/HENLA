# HENLA Claim Boundary

This document defines the strictly enforced boundaries for claims regarding the HENLA architecture.

## 1. Allowed Claims (Validated Internally)
- **Symbolic-Experiential Foundation**: The architecture is built on episodic memory and evidence-based hypergraphs.
- **Architectural Completeness**: The platform implements episodic storage, viability signaling, prediction error detection, deliberative scratchpads, and specialized domain subgraphs.
- **Internal Consistency**: The system successfully passes a chain of over 100 internal benchmarks across 10 roadmap phases.
- **Auditable Reasoning**: Every belief or action can be traced back to its experiential or knowledge-based source in the episode log.
- **Safe by Design**: Implements resource budgeting, uncertainty calibration, and safety redlines within its framework.

## 2. Forbidden Claims (Strictly Prohibited)
- **AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)**: HENLA is a specialized cognitive architecture, not a general human-level mind.
- **Consciousness / Sentience**: HENLA processes data logs of experience; it does not possess subjective awareness.
- **Human-Level Intelligence**: Comparisons to human cognitive breadth are metaphorical and not scientifically validated.
- **Omniscience**: Claims that HENLA "possesses all human knowledge" are false; it possesses an architecture to ingest it.
- **External Audit**: Claims of independent verification are forbidden until such audits are formally completed.

## 3. Pending Claims (Requires External Validation)
- **Robust Generalization**: Performance on unseen, high-complexity repositories not authored by the developers.
- **Real-World Utility**: Effectiveness in diverse production workflows outside controlled simulations.
- **Knowledge Scaling Utility**: The effectiveness of the civilization-scale ingestion layer on massive, noisy datasets.
- **Self-Improvement Impact**: The long-term stability and utility of recursively generated architectural patches in the wild.
